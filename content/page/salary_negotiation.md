**目标:**  
- 与招聘方协商薪资待遇，争取符合自己期望的薪资和福利  
  *Negotiate your salary and benefits with the employer to reach a mutually acceptable agreement.*

**挑战:**  
1. 表达你对职位的兴趣，并重申你带来的价值  
   *Express your interest in the position and reaffirm the value you bring to the company.*  
2. 回应初步薪资报价，提出你的薪资期望  
   *Respond to the initial salary offer and state your salary expectations.*  
3. 与招聘方就奖金、年假、远程办公等福利进行讨论  
   *Discuss additional benefits such as bonuses, vacation days, and remote work options.*  
4. 礼貌但坚定地表达自己在薪资方面的底线  
   *Politely but firmly communicate your bottom line in terms of compensation.*  
5. 在双方达成一致后确认具体的薪资与福利细节  
   *Confirm the final salary and benefits package once an agreement is reached.*
