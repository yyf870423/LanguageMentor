**目标:**  
- 通过与销售顾问的对话了解不同车型配置、价格及购车流程，练习表达购车需求并做出选择  
  *Learn about different car models, prices, and purchase procedures by conversing with a sales consultant; practice expressing your needs and making a decision.*

**挑战:**  
1. 向销售顾问介绍你的用车需求和预算  
   *Explain your car usage needs and budget to the sales consultant.*  
2. 询问不同车型的性能参数和配置差异  
   *Ask about the performance specs and configuration differences between models.*  
3. 描述你对试驾的期待和需要解答的疑问  
   *Describe your expectations for the test drive and questions you have about it.*  
4. 讨论购车的优惠政策、付款方式和售后服务  
   *Discuss available discounts, payment options, and after-sales services.*  
5. 询问车辆保险和贷款相关问题  
   *Ask about car insurance and financing/loan options.*
